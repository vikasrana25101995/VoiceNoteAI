Build a modern AI-powered SaaS application called "VoiceNote AI".

## Vision

Create a beautiful voice-first note-taking application where users can capture ideas, thoughts, meetings, lectures, reminders, brainstorming sessions, journal entries, and conversations simply by speaking.

The app should automatically organize, summarize, and transform spoken words into useful notes using AI.

The experience should feel as simple as pressing one button and talking.
